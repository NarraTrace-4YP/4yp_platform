Risks

From the specification, scored by severity and likelihood out of 5. The rating is the two multiplied together.

# Risk and mitigation S L R Owner
1 Limited access to Blythe computing resources
Schedule jobs between team members, reuse saved LLM outputs, and use smaller local models if necessary. 		4 4 16 All
2 Wiki construction and use exceed the LLM token budget
Measure token use per review and extrapolate. Cap the reconstruction sample within budget. 		5 3 15 Kieran, Mikaeel
3 Scope creep or schedule overrun
Fortnightly progress review; drop lower-priority features if the project falls behind. 		4 3 12 All
4 Integration problems between stages
Document the interfaces between stages, and regularly test the full pipeline on a small sample of reviews. 		4 3 12 Mikaeel
5 Incorrect or unsupported claims in the LLM wiki
Every claim cites its Stage 1 blocks, conflicting accounts are kept with their sources, and claims are checked by an LLM judge validated against human labels, correcting affected pages. 		4 3 12 Arushi
6 The LLM has memorised published DHRs
Prompt models without the source text to see whether they recall case details, include a no-retrieval baseline, use the same model for wiki and RAG, and where possible test on reviews published after the model’s training cut-off. 		4 3 12 Mikaeel
7 Disclosure of identifying information
Keep data and LLM inference on approved systems such as Blythe rather than external APIs, and check anonymised outputs by hand, including regenerated narratives, before public release. 		5 2 10 Arushi, Kieran
8 A team member is unavailable
Share important decisions and code so others can take over work. 		3 3 9 All
9 Not enough independent annotators
Recruit annotators early with the supervisor’s help, and keep evaluation samples manageable. 		3 3 9 All
10 Wiki pages drift after repeated rewrites
Rebuild pages only from cited Stage 1 blocks, measure claim support at 25, 50 and 100 reviews, and log every change. 		3 3 9 Arushi
11 Withheld case metadata or reference answers leak into the wiki, RAG index or prompts
Store evaluation data apart from anything the wiki agent or RAG index can read, and check indexes and prompts for it automatically. 		4 2 8 Rowan
