Reading across domestic homicide reviews

Each Domestic Homicide Review examines one death, so failings that recur only surface when many reviews are compared. NarraTrace builds an LLM wiki over 100 reviews, kept up to date by an agent and linked to its sources, and tests whether it helps researchers find the connections and patterns across them.

Where the project is

Four stages, run in order. Stage 1 turns the reviews into a database the rest of the project can trust; stages 2 and 3 are the heart of the research.

  1. 1

    Corpus preparation

    A database of the reviews in three forms: as published, grouped by section, and mapped to the 2016 report template. Each review is linked to its case details.

    In progressO1
  2. 2

    Knowledge organisation

    The LLM wiki: linked pages on people, agencies, events and recommendations, with every claim citing the passages it came from.

    Not startedO2
  3. 3

    Analysis

    Similar cases, patterns over time, recurring recommendations and lessons, and the critical events behind each case.

    Not startedO3
  4. 4

    Evaluation

    Rebuilding cases from the wiki, comparing the wiki with a standard retrieval baseline, and finding case details the records leave out.

    Not startedO4 to O6
